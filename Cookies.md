## 1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, and scripts operate in a comparable manner, and this policy governs their usage across our digital domains.

---

## 2. Cookies We Use

### A. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Technical)
We use strictly necessary technical cookies to maintain site security, manage form anti-spam telemetry, and record your consent preference via `cookie_consent` (stored for 6 to 12 months), none of which require prior consent under applicable law.

### B. Performance & Analytics Cookies
With your explicit consent, we also utilize Google Analytics cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_<container-id>`, retained for up to 2 years) to collect pseudonymous metrics on visitor traffic, device types, and page interactions with IP anonymization enabled and ad personalization disabled; we do not employ advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking cookies.

---

## 3. Consent

When you first arrive on our Website, an explicit cookie consent banner provides equal prominence to accept or reject non-essential analytics cookies, and Google Analytics does not execute until affirmative consent is given. 

You may change your preferences at any time via the Cookie settings link in our site footer, or withdraw previously granted consent to immediately halt analytics tracking without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

---

## 4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can inspect, restrict, or purge cookies at any time through your browser preferences in Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge. 

Alternatively, you may opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the official [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), noting that blocking strictly necessary cookies may impair specific website functions such as our contact form.
