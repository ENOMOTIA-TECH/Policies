## 2. What We Collect

We collect personal information directly when you reach out to us via our contact form (including your name, email address, message content, and any project details you choose to include), and through direct emails or calls to scope and deliver client engagements. 

When you browse our Website, we automatically collect basic server and security logs (such as IP addresses, timestamps, and user agents for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting) as well as pseudonymous analytics via Google Analytics (including pages viewed, time on site, approximate city or country location, device type, browser, referring page, and pseudonymous cookie IDs to evaluate and improve our Website). 

For invoicing and contractual agreements, we collect business names, billing addresses, contact details, and bank reference information for accounting and legal compliance. We do not collect payment card details through the Website, we do not operate user accounts, logins, or newsletters, and we never knowingly collect information from children under 18 years of age.

For visitors in the EU and UK, we process personal data under established legal bases: we rely on your explicit consent for Google Analytics and non-essential cookies (which you can withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests for maintaining server security logs, responding to client enquiries, and operating our engineering business; contractual necessity for executing proposals, statements of work, and ongoing service delivery; and legal compliance for statutory tax, accounting, and corporate records.

## 9. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered:
- Contact form inquiries without an active client engagement are retained for up to 12 months before deletion.
- Client project records are maintained for the duration of the engagement plus 3 years.
- Invoices and accounting records are kept for the statutory periods mandated by Indian tax and corporate law (generally 6 to 8 years).
- Server security logs are retained for up to 90 days.
- Google Analytics aggregated metrics are retained for up to 14 months. 

Data is securely deleted or permanently anonymized once the applicable retention window expires.
