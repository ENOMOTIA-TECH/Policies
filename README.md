We **do not** process payment cards via the Website, we **do not** require user accounts or logins, and we **never** knowingly collect data from children under 18 years of age.

## 9. Your Statutory Data Protection Rights

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold enforceable statutory rights:

- **Right of Access & Correction:** Obtain a complete copy of your personal data and request correction of inaccurate records.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request the permanent deletion of personal information where no overriding legal obligation exists.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Revoke previously granted consent (such as for analytics) at any time.
- **Right to Nominate (India DPDP Act):** Nominate another individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.
- **Right to Data Portability (EU/UK GDPR):** Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** File a grievance with the **Data Protection Board of India**, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or your local EU supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, contact our Grievance Officer at [contact@enomotiatech.com](mailto:contact@enomotiatech.com). We verify requests and respond within statutory timeframes (30 days).

# Part II: Cookie Policy

## 1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies—such as local storage, session storage, and telemetry beacons—operate in a comparable manner and fall under this governance.

---

## 2. Cookie Categories We Use

### A. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Always Active)
These cookies are required for platform security, form anti-abuse validation, and preserving your cookie preferences. They do not require prior consent under applicable privacy legislation.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Lifespan | Provider |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `cookie_consent` | Stores your accepted or rejected cookie preferences | 6 to 12 months | First-Party |
| `__cf_bm` / Security tokens | Cloudflare bot mitigation and DDoS rate-limiting | Session / 30 min | First-Party / Cloudflare |

### B. Performance & Analytics Cookies (Consent Required)
These cookies activate **only after you grant explicit affirmative consent** via our consent banner.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Lifespan | Provider |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `_ga` | Distinguishes unique visitors anonymously | 2 years | Google Analytics |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Persists session state across page views | 2 years | Google Analytics |

- IP anonymization is enforced at the network ingress.
- Advertising and behavioral retargeting cookies are **completely disabled**.

---

## 3. Consent Architecture

- When visiting [https://enomotiatech.com](https://enomotiatech.com), you receive equal prominence to **Accept Protocol** or choose **Essential Only**.
- No analytics scripts load until you grant explicit consent.
- You can revoke or change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in our global footer.

---

## 4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can inspect, restrict, or purge cookies directly through your browser settings:
- **Google Chrome:** `Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data`
- **Mozilla Firefox:** `Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data`
- **Apple Safari:** `Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data`
- **Microsoft Edge:** `Settings → Cookies and site permissions`

To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites globally, install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
